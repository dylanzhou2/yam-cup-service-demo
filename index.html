MODEL AND VALIDATION SCOPE

Simulation assumptions and recorded data

The viewers replay recorded MuJoCo states with labeled action phases. Each section identifies the model assumptions, controller inputs, and completed checks. Water is a visual overlay in every scene, with a nominal 5 L starting reservoir fill. Cup fill uses the recorded volume estimate, including on dry valve-cycle recordings; probing and sweeping clips leave the cup empty. The tapered fill stays within the cup interior. Fluid dynamics, added water mass, and hardware execution are not validated.
